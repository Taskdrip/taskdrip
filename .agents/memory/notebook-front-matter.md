---
name: Notebook front matter
description: Page-count and numbering rules for optional introductory notebook designs.
---

The physical sequence is optional front blank pages, introductory pages, writing pages, closing pages, then optional end blanks. Front/back blanks and intro/outro designs stay unnumbered; the writing section starts at page 1. The configured writing-page count remains the count of usable writing pages, while physical print totals, validation, KDP eligibility, and cover-spine calculations include every printed page.

**Why:** Editors need a stable writing-page count without undercounting the actual print file; blank sheets and designed front/back matter still consume physical pages.

**How to apply:** When changing notebook page generation or print guidance, map the physical index through the entire front-matter sequence before assigning writing numbers/dates, and count every printed page for KDP calculations.
