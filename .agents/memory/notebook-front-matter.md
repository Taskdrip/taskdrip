---
name: Notebook front matter
description: Page-count and numbering rules for optional introductory notebook designs.
---

The physical sequence is optional front blank pages, introductory pages, writing pages, closing pages, then optional end blanks. When the supplied 2027 planner is enabled, it replaces the standard intro section only; selected closing pages and front/back blanks remain available and print in their normal positions. Front/back blanks and intro/outro designs stay unnumbered; the writing section starts at page 1. The configured writing-page count remains the count of usable writing pages, while physical print totals, validation, KDP eligibility, and cover-spine calculations include every printed page.

**Why:** Editors need a stable writing-page count without undercounting the actual print file; blank sheets and designed front/back matter still consume physical pages. The user also requires journals with imported planner pages to retain optional closing designs and intentional blanks for KDP output.

**How to apply:** When changing notebook page generation or print guidance, map the physical index through the entire front-matter sequence before assigning writing numbers/dates, preserve selected closing pages and both blank-page controls when imported planner pages are on, and count every printed page for KDP calculations.
