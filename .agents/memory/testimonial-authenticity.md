---
name: Testimonial authenticity
description: Guidance for adding customer proof to conversion pages without presenting fabricated endorsements as real.
---

Use verified customer quotes and approved headshots for published testimonials. Until those assets are supplied, label any example copy and generated avatars as illustrative rather than implying real client experience.

**Why:** Conversion improvements should build trust without creating misleading endorsements or misrepresenting stock/generated portraits as customers.

**How to apply:** Keep illustrative founder perspectives clearly labeled in the UI, then replace them with attributable reviews, role/company details, and approved images when the owner provides them.
