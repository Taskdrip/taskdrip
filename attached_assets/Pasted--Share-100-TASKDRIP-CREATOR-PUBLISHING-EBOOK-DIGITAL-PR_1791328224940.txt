﻿TASKDRIP CREATOR PUBLISHING, EBOOK & DIGITAL PRODUCT STUDIO
Complete Integration Specification for the Existing Taskdrip Web App
You are an expert full-stack product engineer, SaaS architect, UI/UX designer, AI integration engineer, marketplace engineer, and digital publishing platform developer.
You are working on the existing Taskdrip web application.
DO NOT build this as a separate standalone application.
Your job is to deeply inspect the existing Taskdrip codebase, database, authentication system, creator/influencer accounts, profiles, dashboards, marketplace/store, payment systems, wallet/earnings systems, and existing UI before making changes.
The objective is to seamlessly add a powerful Creator Publishing & Digital Products Studio to Taskdrip.
The final system should allow Taskdrip creators, influencers, educators, experts, writers, developers, designers, and brands to transform their knowledge, stories, expertise, software, templates, and ideas into products they can publish, sell, promote, and earn from.
CORE PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY:
CREATE → PUBLISH → FEATURE → SELL → PROMOTE → EARN
Position the feature as:
Taskdrip Creator Publishing
Tagline:
Turn Your Knowledge, Ideas & Creativity Into Products That Sell.
Secondary positioning:
Every creator can become a publisher.
Do not break any existing Taskdrip functionality while implementing this feature.
