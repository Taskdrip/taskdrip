# **Give a Young African a Chance to Build the Future**

**Every day, somewhere in Africa, a young person wakes up with talent, ambition and dreams — but no computer, no reliable internet, no mentor, and no opportunity to learn.**

My name is **Abraham Tahbat**. I am a lawyer, entrepreneur and web developer, and the founder of **Breedskool Galaxy Ltd.**

For years, I have worked in technology and watched how access to digital skills can completely change a person's future. Today, I want to help make that opportunity available to young Africans who are being left behind.

## **The talent is there. The opportunity isn't.**

Imagine being a young person in a rural community.

You have never owned a computer.

Your phone may be shared with other members of your family — or you may not have one at all.

Internet access is unreliable or completely unavailable.

There is no technology centre nearby. No coding teacher. No AI mentor. No professional network to guide you.

You may be intelligent, creative and determined, but the modern digital economy can feel like a world you are simply not allowed to enter.

For young people living in communities affected by poverty, unemployment, displacement, insecurity and economic hardship, this gap can become a cycle that lasts for generations.

**We believe where someone is born should not determine whether they get a chance to participate in the future.**

Through our **Breedskool training initiative, powered by Taskdrip.online**, we want to bring practical technology education and opportunity closer to young people who need it most.

Our goal is to provide training in areas such as:

* Artificial Intelligence and AI tools
* Web development
* Digital skills
* Blockchain technology , crypto and Airdrops
* Social media management
* Content creation
* Digital marketing
* Entrepreneurship
* Freelancing and remote work
* Emerging technologies

But **training alone is not enough.**

A young person cannot learn web development without a computer.

They cannot practise AI without access to technology.

They cannot participate in the global digital economy without internet access.

And sometimes, they simply need someone to say:

**“I believe you can do this.”**

That is why Breedskool is designed around **skills, access, equipment, mentorship and opportunity.**
